# UI follow-ups

The high-priority findings from the September 2026 usability review have been
implemented. Two lower-priority improvements remain:

- At widths around 800 px, the idle media-source card becomes taller than its
  content warrants. Keep the primary folder action visible while making the
  source count and status more compact.
- Cards in Activity are clickable and have accessible names, but they do not
  show a visible `Open details` or `Manage` action. Make the navigation intent
  explicit without duplicating import controls in Activity.

These are presentation improvements. They do not block scanning, planning,
importing, recovery, or backup workflows.
